**A Netflix-exclusive special episode of India's Got Latent Season 2 dropped on September 4, bringing Varun Dhawan and Medha Shankr in as guests alongside Samay Raina and panelist Sharon Verma. The standout moment came from stand-up comedian Vijay Poriya, whose dog-themed act split the panel's reaction but still walked away with a Rs 1 lakh cash prize.**

## Why did this episode skip YouTube entirely?

Unlike the show's usual releases, which typically go out simultaneously across YouTube and Netflix, this particular special was Netflix-only. That's a notable shift for a show that built its entire reputation as a YouTube-first format before season 2 added Netflix as a second platform — a fully exclusive drop signals the streamer is being given content the show considers valuable enough to use as a subscriber draw, not just a simulcast partner.

## What did Varun Dhawan actually bring to the episode?

Varun Dhawan joined as a special guest alongside Medha Shankr, marking his first appearance on the show. Speaking about the experience, Dhawan said comedy is a genre he loves performing and watching, and that Samay Raina's brand of comedy is unpredictable enough that he wanted to experience it live. That's a fairly standard promotional line for a guest appearance, but it's consistent with how other mainstream Bollywood names — Alia Bhatt and Sharvari Wagh at the season premiere, for instance — have framed their own appearances on the show: less about performing comedy themselves, more about being present for and reacting to Raina's format.

## Who was Vijay Poriya, and why did his act split the panel?

Vijay Poriya is a stand-up comedian known primarily for performing at comedy shows in Gujarat, and this episode marked a notably bigger stage for him. His act built around dogs as the central subject reportedly received a mixed response from the panel — not a unanimous rave, but enough to win him the episode's cash prize regardless. That gap between "mixed panel reaction" and "still walked away with Rs 1 lakh" is worth sitting with: it's a reminder that on this show, panel commentary and the actual outcome for a contestant aren't always perfectly aligned, and a polarizing act can still be a winning one if it's memorable enough.

Fan reaction online leaned enthusiastic regardless of the panel's mixed take, with viewers specifically calling out Sharon Verma's presence on the panel as a highlight and speculating she's becoming a consistent fixture on the show rather than a rotating guest.

## What has the fan reaction to the episode actually looked like?

Online response has leaned enthusiastic, even with the panel's own reaction to Poriya's act landing as mixed. Comment sections on the episode have been dominated by two recurring reactions: viewers calling the episode a satisfying watch overall, and a specific wave of comments predicting Sharon Verma's segments from the episode are set to travel especially well as standalone clips once they start circulating more widely. That kind of clip-specific prediction has become a fairly reliable signal on this show — panelists who generate that reaction early tend to get pulled into future episodes more regularly, which tracks with Verma's increasingly frequent appearances across season 2's panel rotations this year.

## How does this fit into the show's wider season 2 run?

This Netflix special landed just two days before the show's regular episode 6, which released September 6 with a considerably more attention-grabbing panel lineup: Rakhi Sawant, Ashneer Grover, Kushagra Srivastava, and Balraj Ghai alongside Samay Raina. Between a Bollywood-guest Netflix exclusive and a big-personality mainline episode landing within days of each other, season 2 is clearly leaning into a release cadence that mixes formats deliberately — [not unlike the pattern we noted](/posts/indias-got-latent-episode-5-orry-archana-puran-singh-recap) with episode 5's mismatched Orry and Archana Puran Singh panel a few weeks earlier, where the show seems to be testing how far it can stretch its panel combinations while keeping the core format recognizable.

That pattern extends further back into the season too. The show's earlier members-only bonus episodes — including one where Samay Raina roasted vlogger Saurav Joshi over the contrast between his car and his haircut — followed a similar logic of using a smaller, off-format episode to generate a specific viral clip rather than a full standard release. A Netflix-exclusive special with a Bollywood A-lister fits that same underlying strategy, just aimed at a different platform and a different audience than a YouTube members-only drop would reach.
