Orry sat on the India's Got Latent panel for episode 5. Not as a guest bit, not for one segment — for the whole episode, next to Archana Puran Singh, Nishant Suri, and Sharon Verma, judging comedy acts alongside three people whose actual jobs involve judging comedy acts.

## The booking logic, and where it breaks

The casting logic is obvious enough. Orry brings an audience that doesn't necessarily watch stand-up-adjacent YouTube shows, and a panel built entirely from comedians risks feeling insular after two seasons. Mixing in a name from outside the comedy world is the same move the show has made before, and it's usually worked — Alia Bhatt and Sharvari didn't need comic timing to anchor the season 2 premiere, because they were there to be interviewed and reacted to, not to critique.

The judging panel is a different job. A panelist's value on this show isn't presence, it's read — can they tell when a bit is actually landing versus performing confidence, can they push back on a contestant's self-score with something sharper than a vibe. Suri and Verma do that because it's their craft. Archana Puran Singh does it because she's spent decades reading a room professionally. Orry's public career has never required that skill, and nothing about a party-and-paparazzi platform builds it by accident.

## What actually gets lost

None of this makes Orry's presence a disaster — early clips from the episode suggest the mismatch itself became part of the entertainment, which is very on-brand for a show that's always found comedy in panels that don't quite fit together. But there's a difference between a panel mismatch that produces good television and a panel mismatch that quietly lowers the bar for what counts as judging. If three-quarters of a panel's feedback is craft-informed and one quarter is vibes, contestants aren't getting evaluated by the same standard four times over — they're getting evaluated three times and reacted to once.

For a show that built its identity on unfiltered, competent roasting, that's worth watching over multiple episodes, not just this one. One influencer seat is a casting choice. A pattern of them is a format change nobody announced.
