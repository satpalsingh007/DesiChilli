**India's Got Latent's third members-only bonus episode brings together internet personality Deepak Kalal, stand-up comedian Ravi Gupta, and Agu Stanley alongside Samay Raina and his regular co-host Balraj Singh Ghai. It's available exclusively to paying YouTube members, not on Netflix or the free YouTube feed — and it landed in the same stretch as [Season 2 Episode 7 with Nawazuddin Siddiqui and Bhuvan Bam](/posts/indias-got-latent-s2-ep7-ft-nawazuddin-siddiqui-bhuvan-bam-mukesh-chhabra-kaustubh-agarwal).**

## What's actually in this bonus episode, and who's on the panel?

The panel leans deliberately toward chaos rather than polish. Deepak Kalal, one of the internet's more unpredictable personalities, known for content that swings between genuinely funny and completely off the rails, headlines the guest lineup. Ravi Gupta brings a more grounded, traditional stand-up sensibility to balance him out, while Agu Stanley adds his own flavor of internet-native comedy into the mix. With Balraj back alongside Samay as co-host, the combination pulls the format closer to the show's original, rawer roots than the larger celebrity panels the mainline season has been leaning into lately.

That contrast is worth sitting with for a second. Season 2's numbered episodes have increasingly featured recognizable mainstream names — Alia Bhatt and Sharvari at the premiere, Varun Dhawan in a Netflix special, Rakhi Sawant and Ashneer Grover on the most recent numbered episode. The bonus-episode track has been running in almost the opposite direction, pulling from internet-native personalities and smaller, looser panels that feel less produced and more like the show's earliest YouTube-only days.

## Who is Deepak Kalal, and why does his presence matter for this episode's tone?

For readers less familiar with him, Deepak Kalal's internet reputation is built almost entirely on unpredictability rather than traditional comedy craft — he's known for content and public appearances that regularly veer into territory other creators would consider too chaotic or unscripted to air, which is exactly the quality that makes him a natural fit for the bonus-episode format specifically. Where the mainline show's celebrity panels tend to produce moments through practiced comic timing and rehearsed critique, Kalal's appeal comes from genuine unpredictability — the sense that literally anything could happen once he's given a microphone and an opinion to react to.

That's a meaningfully different comedic engine than what's driving the mainline season right now, and it's part of why long-time fans of the original 2024 season have specifically flagged this bonus-episode track as feeling closer to what made the show popular in the first place. Smaller panels, rawer humor, and reactions that feel genuinely off the cuff rather than produced for a bigger, more mainstream audience — that's the pitch for this format, and pairing it with someone like Kalal is about as direct a fulfillment of that pitch as the show has made this season.

## Where can you actually watch this episode?

This is the detail worth being precise about, since it trips people up every time a new bonus episode drops: it's available exclusively on Samay Raina's official YouTube channel, and only to viewers with an active paid channel membership, currently priced at around Rs 59 a month. It is not on Netflix, and it is not available to regular, non-member YouTube subscribers. If you've been checking Netflix or your normal YouTube subscriptions feed and not finding it, that's expected — this episode was never released there.

## How does this fit into the season's wider bonus episode pattern?

This is the third bonus episode of season 2, following earlier installments that included [Samay Raina's roast of vlogger Saurav Joshi](/posts/samay-raina-roasts-saurav-joshi-haircut-bonus-episode) over the gap between his car and his haircut, alongside guests Badshah and Harsh Limbachiyaa. That earlier episode established the format's basic shape: smaller panels than the mainline show, guests pulled from outside the usual comedy circuit, and content built to produce a specific, highly shareable moment rather than a full slate of contestant performances. This third bonus episode, with Deepak Kalal's specific brand of unpredictability at the center, looks built for exactly the same purpose.

For fans trying to keep track of the difference between the two tracks: mainline numbered episodes (now through episode 7) release simultaneously on Netflix and YouTube for free, following a roughly biweekly schedule. Bonus episodes release only to paying YouTube members, on no fixed schedule, and tend to be announced just days ahead of release through Samay's own Instagram rather than any formal promotional campaign.
