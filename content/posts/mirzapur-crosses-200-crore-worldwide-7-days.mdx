**Mirzapur: The Movie has crossed the Rs 200 crore mark worldwide within its first week of release, per Filmibeat, with an India net collection of roughly Rs 148 crore by Day 7. The film's steadiest achievement isn't the milestone itself — it's how it got there, holding its ground through the weekdays instead of dropping off sharply once its opening weekend crowd had already shown up.**

## How did Mirzapur actually get to Rs 200 crore this fast?

The film opened on September 4 to roughly Rs 25 crore net on Day 1 — [among the biggest Hindi Day 1 openings of 2026](/posts/mirzapur-opens-rs-24-75-crore-4th-biggest-2026) — and grew through its opening weekend rather than front-loading its numbers into a single big Friday. That pattern continued past the weekend too: rather than the sharp Monday drop that usually follows a strong opening, the film kept collecting more than Rs 10 crore net on its seventh day alone, well into what's normally the quietest stretch of a film's opening week. Sacnilk's tracking put the film's India net collection at around Rs 138 crore through Day 6, with Filmibeat's Day 7 update pushing the domestic net closer to Rs 148 crore and the worldwide gross past Rs 200 crore.

## Why does holding steady through weekdays actually matter?

Because it's the exact pattern that separates a genuine hit from a front-loaded opening built on hype alone. [Toxic's own opening week](/posts/toxic-94-percent-monday-crash-bigger-story-than-opening) is still the clearest recent counterexample: a massive Day 1 number that collapsed by roughly 94% within days, a fall trade press compared to Thugs of Hindostan. Mirzapur doing close to the opposite — maintaining double-digit daily collections a full week into its run — is a much stronger signal that word of mouth is genuinely carrying the film, rather than the opening weekend simply being the film's entire commercial story.

## Where is the film performing strongest?

The film's regional strength has stayed consistent with what showed up in its opening weekend numbers — particularly strong turnout across the mass centres of Uttar Pradesh and Bihar, the same belt where the original Mirzapur streaming series built its most loyal fanbase. That regional consistency across a full week, rather than just an opening-weekend spike, suggests the film's core audience isn't just curious streaming fans buying one ticket out of novelty — they're the same audience the show built over three seasons, showing up in numbers that are holding rather than fading.

## What does this mean for the film's original theatrical bet?

[We flagged the core risk](/posts/mirzapur-bets-fans-will-pay-for-what-was-free) before the film even released: could a streaming audience already paying for a Prime Video subscription actually be convinced to pay full theatrical prices for more of the same world. A week in, the answer looks like a genuinely confident yes — not just because the opening numbers were strong, but because the audience kept returning through an entire week that usually separates a real hit from a one-weekend curiosity spike. Crossing Rs 200 crore worldwide in seven days puts Mirzapur comfortably among the more durable success stories of 2026's Hindi box office, regardless of how it ultimately stacks up against the year's very biggest openers in absolute scale.
