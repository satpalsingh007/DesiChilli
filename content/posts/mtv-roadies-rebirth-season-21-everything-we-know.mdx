**MTV Roadies REBIRTH — officially confirmed as the franchise's 21st season — was announced on August 28, 2026, with Rannvijay Singha returning as host and a phoenix-themed logo signaling a full reset for the 20-plus-year-old format.** Here's a clear line between what's locked in and what's still speculation.

## The headline news: Rannvijay is back

Rannvijay Singha's association with Roadies goes back to 2004, first as a contestant and gang leader, later as host across multiple seasons through 2020, and again from 2025 with Roadies XX: Double Cross. His return for REBIRTH was announced alongside the logo reveal, and in his own comments he described the show as "an emotion," not just a hosting gig — leaning into the idea that this season is positioned as a homecoming for the franchise as much as for him personally.

## Why "REBIRTH," and what the phoenix means

The season's identity centers on a phoenix, a symbol the makers explicitly tied to renewal. Combined with Rannvijay's return, the branding reads as an intentional pivot back toward the show's earlier identity — raw, confrontational, less polished — after recent seasons experimented with format tweaks and celebrity-heavy panels.

## What's confirmed right now

- **Season number**: 21st edition of MTV Roadies.
- **Title**: Roadies REBIRTH.
- **Host**: Rannvijay Singha.
- **Audition cities**: Delhi, Chandigarh, Kolkata, and Pune, all in September 2026.
- **Streaming home**: Expected to continue on JioHotstar, in line with the platform's recent seasons.

## What's still unconfirmed

- **Exact audition dates** for each of the four cities.
- **Gang leaders** for the new season — no names have been officially revealed yet.
- **Judging panel** for the audition rounds.
- **Season format twists**, though given the franchise's pattern, some kind of alliance-or-betrayal mechanic is close to guaranteed.
