**Mirzapur: The Movie opened in theatres on September 4 to an estimated Rs 24.75 crore net across India, according to trade tracker Sacnilk, putting it among the biggest Hindi Day 1 openings of 2026 — behind only Dhurandhar 2, Toxic, and Border 2.**

## How big was Mirzapur's opening day, really?

Figures vary slightly depending on the tracker — Sacnilk reported Rs 24.75 crore net with a gross of roughly Rs 29.70 crore across 11,135 shows, while Bollywood Hungama's live tracking put the net figure marginally lower at Rs 24.47 crore. Either way, the film comfortably cleared Awarapan 2's reported Rs 22 crore opening from a few weeks earlier, while landing behind Border 2's Rs 30 crore Day 1. Early estimates can shift as final numbers are confirmed over the next day or two, so treat the exact decimal points loosely.

## Where did the film perform strongest?

Occupancy data points to a clear regional story: Lucknow recorded around 71% overall occupancy, well above the roughly 48% seen in the Delhi NCR region and 50% in Jaipur. That pattern lines up with where Mirzapur's world — built around a fictionalized version of Uttar Pradesh's power politics — has always resonated hardest, with Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, and Delhi driving a disproportionate share of the opening-day turnout compared to a more typical pan-India spread.

## What does this opening actually prove?

A Rs 24-25 crore opening for a film built entirely on a streaming show's fanbase is a genuinely strong signal, and it's the number [we flagged as the real test](/posts/mirzapur-bets-fans-will-pay-for-what-was-free) when the film's theatrical bet was first announced: could a Prime Video audience be convinced to pay for a ticket. Day one says yes, decisively. Whether that opening turns into a healthy multi-week run instead of a front-loaded spike is the more interesting question — and given how sharply Toxic's own opening-week momentum reversed just days earlier, Mirzapur's next few days matter more than this first number does.
