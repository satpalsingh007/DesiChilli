**Speculation about a possible romance between Kanika Mann and Kushal Tanwar (Gullu) started building inside the Bigg Boss 20 house after Isha Rikhi and Arishfa Khan noticed Gullu blushing while talking about Kanika, and began teasing the two on camera. Kanika addressed it directly, telling Isha she isn't interested in a romantic storyline and wants to focus on playing the game individually.**

## How did the Kanika-Gullu speculation actually start?

The rumours trace back to a specific moment rather than a slow-building storyline. Isha Rikhi and Arishfa Khan reportedly caught Gullu blushing during a conversation that touched on Kanika Mann, and decided to lean into it by teasing him further about her in front of the house. That moment got picked up widely once the episode aired, with viewers online quickly speculating whether Bigg Boss 20 was about to get its first "couple" storyline of the season — a narrative arc the franchise has leaned on heavily in past seasons.

## What did Kanika Mann actually say about it?

When Isha directly raised the subject with Kanika, she was clear about where she stood. Kanika denied having romantic feelings for Gullu and asked Isha not to build a love story around the two of them. She's also said publicly that her focus inside the house is the competition itself, not a relationship track, and that she isn't seeking anything romantic during her time on the show. Kanika has previously spoken about a four-year relationship that ended and took her roughly two years to move past — context that arguably makes her firm response here less surprising than it might otherwise read.

## Why does Kanika Mann already have extra influence in the house?

Kanika's position in this storyline is worth understanding alongside a separate detail from the premiere: she was the very first contestant to enter the Bigg Boss 20 house, and was immediately given a special "Puppet Master" privilege by Bigg Boss during her confession room introduction — a twist that gives her some degree of influence over contestants who entered after her. Exactly how that privilege gets used mechanically hasn't been fully detailed yet, but it puts her in a genuinely different starting position than most of this season's cast, independent of whatever does or doesn't happen with Gullu.

## What else happened in the house alongside this storyline?

The same stretch of episodes that produced the Kanika-Gullu teasing also included a separate disagreement between Olympic boxer Mary Kom and Rhiti Tiwari over kitchen duties, and continued movement in the season's captaincy race, which has narrowed down to three contenders: Gullu, Aasif Khan, and Yung DSA — the same three names [we flagged in our Week 1 recap](/posts/bigg-boss-20-week-1-recap-mahhi-vij-first-nominations) as central to the season's opening captaincy task and its lifeline-trading twist. ## How does this compare to past Bigg Boss "couple" storylines?

The franchise has leaned on romantic pairings as a recurring narrative device for years, and viewers picking up on the smallest hint of chemistry — a blush, a teasing comment from fellow contestants — has become a familiar pattern almost every season, regardless of whether either contestant involved actually intends to pursue anything. What makes this particular moment worth tracking isn't necessarily that it's unusual; it's that Kanika shut it down this directly and this early, rather than letting the ambiguity play out for weeks the way some past "will they, won't they" storylines have been allowed to run. Whether producers and editing choices continue leaning into the pairing anyway, regardless of her stated position, is worth watching over the next few episodes — the show has a track record of keeping a storyline alive in the edit even after a contestant has denied it on camera.

As of this writing, neither Kanika nor Gullu has said anything beyond Kanika's on-air denial, so treat any further "confirmed couple" framing elsewhere as reported speculation rather than something either contestant has actually confirmed.
