**Rapper Badshah and Punjabi actor Isha Rikhi have announced their separation, five months after marrying in a private ceremony this past March. Both shared identical statements on their Instagram Stories confirming the decision was mutual, and asked fans and media to respect their privacy going forward.**

## What did Badshah and Isha Rikhi actually say?

Badshah posted a brief statement saying the two had mutually decided to part ways and move forward on separate paths, adding that the decision was made with mutual respect and asking that there be no further speculation about the reasons behind it. Isha Rikhi shared essentially the same wording on her own account. Neither statement went into further detail, and both were framed as the only comment either of them intended to make on the matter.

## How did their relationship and marriage actually unfold?

Badshah and Isha reportedly dated for around four years before marrying in a private ceremony in March 2026 — a wedding that was initially kept out of the spotlight, though photos later surfaced online through Isha's mother, Poonam Rikhi. Isha herself confirmed the marriage publicly in June, several months after the private ceremony had already taken place. Speculation about trouble in the marriage began circulating in July, after Isha shared a cryptic post on social media, though neither party addressed those rumors directly until this week's joint announcement.

## Why does this news carry extra weight right now?

The timing gives this story an unusual second layer: Isha Rikhi is currently a contestant on Bigg Boss 20, which premiered September 6 and is airing nightly while this personal news breaks outside the house. Contestants inside the Bigg Boss house are typically kept isolated from real-world news during their run, which raises a genuine question the show hasn't addressed yet — whether and when Isha will actually learn about the announcement being made in her name, given that the statement had to have been shared with her cooperation before she entered isolation, or arranged separately while she's inside.

For Badshah, this marks the end of his second marriage. He was previously married to Jasmine Masih, with that relationship ending in divorce in 2020.

## What made this relationship's public timeline unusual?

The way this marriage played out publicly is worth noting on its own. Most celebrity weddings this high-profile are either fully staged for media coverage or kept entirely private with no public confirmation at all. This one sat somewhere in between: a genuinely private ceremony in March, followed by unofficial photos surfacing through a family member rather than the couple themselves, followed by Isha's own confirmation months later in June, and now a joint divorce announcement barely three months after that confirmation. That's a compressed and unusually public arc for a relationship that both parties initially tried to keep out of the spotlight entirely.

## What's still unconfirmed?

Neither Badshah nor Isha has said anything publicly beyond their matching separation statements, and both have explicitly asked that speculation about the cause be avoided. Any reporting suggesting a specific reason for the split beyond "mutual decision" should be treated as unconfirmed unless one of them addresses it directly themselves.
