**On India's Got Latent's August 10 bonus episode, Samay Raina roasted vlogger Saurav Joshi over the gap between his luxury-car content and his haircut — a "crore car, ten-rupee haircut" framing that became the episode's most-shared moment.** The panel also brought together rapper Badshah and comedian-writer Harsh Limbachiyaa, and like previous bonus installments this season, the episode released first exclusively to paying YouTube members, with wider public access following later.

## Who was on the bonus episode panel?

Bonus episodes have quietly become one of the more interesting formats within season 2 — smaller panels, less structure, and guests pulled from outside the usual comedy circuit. This one leaned into that by pairing Badshah, a major mainstream music name, with Joshi, one of India's biggest vlogging-format YouTubers, neither of whom come from a stand-up or sketch comedy background the way most of the show's regular panelists do.

That gap is exactly where the episode's most-shared moment came from. Raina used the pairing to needle Joshi over the contrast between his lifestyle-vlog persona — built around expensive cars and high-end purchases — and his haircut, framing it as a "crore car, ten-rupee haircut" kind of joke. It's the same style of pointed-but-affectionate roasting Raina has used on guests throughout the show's run, aimed at a visible contradiction rather than anything personal.

## Is this typical of season 2's bonus episodes?

This wasn't an isolated moment. The bonus episode immediately before this one, featuring dancer-actor Raghav Juyal, comedian Munawar Faruqui, creator Niharika NM and comedian Rohan Joshi, followed a similar shape — guests promoting other projects (Juyal and NM were there while promoting *Bhai Tera Star Hai*) getting drawn into the same kind of good-natured roasting that's become the bonus format's signature. Faruqui in particular was on the receiving end of a joke from Raina comparing his relationship history to Aamir Khan's.

Taken together, the bonus episodes are functioning almost like a release valve for the show — lower stakes than the [mainline biweekly episodes](/posts/indias-got-latent-episode-5-orry-archana-puran-singh-recap), built around chemistry and off-the-cuff exchanges rather than full contestant slates, and clearly designed to travel well as short clips. They're also, arguably, where [most of season 2's remaining edge now lives](/posts/season-2-played-it-safe-not-a-compliment).

## Where can you watch the bonus episode?

Worth flagging directly: the bonus episode is a members-only release on Samay Raina's YouTube channel, meaning full access requires an active paid membership. Unauthorized re-uploads on platforms like Dailymotion have circulated for earlier bonus episodes, but quality and completeness on those are unreliable, and they don't support the show. If you want the full, unedited cut — including whatever else Raina, Badshah, and Limbachiyaa got into beyond the haircut bit — the official channel is the only place to see it properly. For what it's worth, we think [gating the show's best moments this way is a mistake](/posts/latent-members-only-episodes-hurting-show).
