**Bigg Boss 20's second Weekend Ka Vaar, which aired September 19-20, packed in three separate storylines that are usually spread across multiple episodes: a captaincy-linked twist that stripped nominees down to a single lifeline each, the season's first reported eviction, and a staged health scare from Salman Khan himself aimed squarely at contestant Qazi Touqeer. Here's everything that happened, and what's confirmed versus still developing.**

## How did three contestants end up with just one lifeline each?

The episode's setup traces back to the [punishment nomination from earlier in the week](/posts/bigg-boss-20-nomination-task-cancelled-punishment), where Aasif Khan, Rohed Khan, and Tanmay "ScoutOP" Singh were directly nominated after Bigg Boss caught them discussing nominations in advance with house captain Yung DSA. All three entered the danger zone with two lifelines apiece under the season's "Tathastu" twist.

That cushion narrowed fast. During a captaincy-linked task, the contenders for captain were each given the option to strip a lifeline from one of the three nominees. Gullu targeted ScoutOP, Kanika Mann targeted Rohed Khan, and Mahhi Vij targeted Aasif Khan — leaving all three nominees down to a single remaining lifeline heading into the audience vote, and making this week's voting result meaningfully higher-stakes than a standard nomination round.

## Who actually got evicted this week?

Multiple outlets, including India TV News, Sunday Guardian, and NewsBytes, are reporting that Rohed Khan received the fewest votes among the three nominees and has become the season's first reported eviction. Aasif Khan and ScoutOP are both reported to have survived. Several of these reports specifically flag Rohed's limited screen presence and light involvement in house tasks as a likely factor in the result — a pattern that's fairly common for contestants who haven't built a strong on-air storyline or alliance base by their second week.

One important caveat worth repeating clearly: as of this writing, several of these reports note that the makers had not yet confirmed Rohed's exit on air at the time of publication. Bigg Boss eviction results are traditionally announced by Salman Khan directly during the episode itself, and unofficial reports — even when they align across multiple outlets — sometimes get ahead of the actual broadcast confirmation. Treat this as highly likely but not yet fully official until the on-air announcement airs or is directly confirmed by Colors or JioHotstar.

If Rohed's exit is confirmed, it would mark the season's first departure from a house that began with sixteen contestants spanning television, music, sport, and content creation — including Mary Kom, Mahhi Vij, Amrapali Dubey, Kanika Mann, Aasif Khan, ScoutOP, Yung DSA, Love Gill, Uditi Singh, Qazi Touqeer, Aman Gandhi, Isha Rikhi, Arishfa Khan, Gullu, and Rhiti Tiwari. Losing any contestant this early tends to reshuffle house alliances meaningfully, since a cast this size hasn't had much time yet to settle into stable groupings.

## What happened with Salman Khan's staged chest pain?

[We've covered this in detail separately](/posts/salman-khan-fake-chest-pain-lesson-qazi-touqeer), but the short version: a promo clip from this same episode showed Salman clutching his chest and asking for water, which briefly alarmed fans before he clarified on Instagram that it was staged. The target of the demonstration was Qazi Touqeer, who'd been secretly faking illness inside the house — including a moment where he asked loudly for medicines after a task, worrying his fellow housemates, before privately telling both the medical staff and the cameras that it had all been an act.

Salman's own explanation was that the staged moment was meant to show Qazi what it feels like to have people around you genuinely frightened for your health without knowing it's a performance. He also directly acknowledged the irony that the promo's editing made his demonstration look like the exact behavior he was trying to criticize.

## Was there tension between Salman and Aasif Khan too?

Promotional material for the episode also flagged a separate moment — Salman reportedly addressing Aasif Khan directly and critically during the episode, alongside the broader nomination fallout. Specific details of that exchange weren't available in the sources reviewed for this piece, so treat it as a confirmed talking point from the episode's promos without a fully reported account of what was actually said. We'll update this if a fuller breakdown becomes available.

## What happened with Salman's shirtless moment and paparazzi comments?

This same episode also produced its most-discussed segment: Salman removing his shirt on camera to respond to trolling about his own age, hair, and weight, before turning his focus to paparazzi photography practices he called inappropriate — specifically, shots deliberately framed around actresses' bodies as they enter and exit cars. He extended the point to the recent trolling directed at Katrina Kaif over her post-pregnancy appearance, and it didn't take long for former Bigg Boss 7 contestant Sofia Hayat to publicly call him hypocritical over it, citing his own past comments about her body during her season. [We've covered that whole thread in detail separately](/posts/salman-khan-shirtless-slams-paparazzi-hip-shots), since it's substantial enough to warrant its own breakdown.

## How do all three storylines connect?

Looking at the episode as a whole, there's a clear throughline: Week 2 has been built almost entirely around punishment and consequence, from the cancelled nomination task through the lifeline-stripping twist to Salman's own staged illness aimed at correcting Qazi's behavior. That's a notably heavier, more disciplinary tone than the show's opening week, which leaned more on romance speculation and house banter. Whether that tone continues into Week 3 or the show pivots back toward lighter storylines is worth watching, especially with Rohed's reported exit reshaping house dynamics for whoever remains.
