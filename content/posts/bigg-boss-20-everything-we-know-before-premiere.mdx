The season is built around a reported "Old vs New" theme and a redesigned house from new art directors — while every contestant list circulating online remains unconfirmed.** Makers are billing it as a landmark 20th season, and here's a straight rundown of what's actually settled versus what's still rumor.

"Reported" here means outlets are citing sources close to production rather than an announcement from Colors or JioHotstar. That distinction is not pedantry — format details in this bucket get reworked or quietly dropped most years, and contestant lists in particular have a poor track record.

## Who designed the Bigg Boss 20 house?

For the first time in years, the iconic Bigg Boss set won't be built by longtime designer Omung Kumar and his wife Vanita Garud Kumar. Art directors Kanchan and Rupali — who previously designed the set for *The 50* — have reportedly taken over for season 20, meaning the house itself will likely look meaningfully different from what longtime viewers are used to.

## Where will Bigg Boss 20 stream?

Reports indicate the new season will follow a digital-first model, with episodes streaming on JioHotstar before their Colors TV broadcast — a shift that mirrors the streaming-first push seen across other reality formats this year, including [India's Got Latent's own dual-platform approach](/posts/season-2-played-it-safe-not-a-compliment).

## What is the Bigg Boss 20 theme?

The headline format twist this season is reportedly an "Old vs New" structure, mixing fresh celebrity contestants with former Bigg Boss participants competing under the same roof. Alongside that, reports point to an "Extra Jeevan Daan" twist — effectively a second-life mechanic giving eliminated contestants a shot at re-entering the house through additional tasks. We've [broken down exactly how that twist is expected to work](/posts/what-is-extra-jeevan-daan-bigg-boss-20-explained) in a separate explainer, and argued that the "Old vs New" theme is [less a bold format swing than a casting admission](/posts/bigg-boss-20-old-vs-new-cast-admits-problem).

## How does the Bigg Boss format actually work?

If you're coming to the show fresh for its 20th season, the weekly machinery matters more than any individual twist:

**The house is sealed.** Contestants live together with no phones, no internet, and no contact with the outside world, under continuous camera coverage. Bigg Boss himself is a disembodied voice issuing instructions from a control room contestants never see.

**Tasks drive everything.** Bigg Boss assigns tasks that determine ration, luxury budget, immunity, and captaincy. They're the engine of most conflict, because they force contestants into competition with people they also need as allies.

**Captaincy grants power.** Each week produces a captain with authority inside the house and, typically, protection from that week's nominations.

**Nominations are internal, evictions are external.** Housemates choose who goes up for eviction; the public decides which of them leaves. That split is the show's central tension — you have to survive your housemates' politics *and* stay likeable to an audience you can't see. If you plan to take part, our guide to [how Bigg Boss 20 voting works](/posts/how-to-vote-bigg-boss-20-explained) covers the window, the allocation, and the fake voting sites that show up every season.

**The host is a weekly reckoning.** Salman Khan's weekend episodes function as a review of the week's behaviour, and they've historically shaped public opinion as much as they report it.

**It runs for months.** A full season stretches across a long stretch of weeks, which is why endurance and social stamina tend to matter more than early strategy.

## Why does a 20th season matter?

Twenty seasons is an unusual run for any format, and it creates a specific problem: the audience knows the game better than most contestants do. Viewers can identify a manufactured fight, a strategic alliance, or a producer-driven storyline within the first fortnight, which steadily erodes the thing the show is selling — the sense that you're watching people behave naturally.

That's the context for both reported twists this year. "Old vs New" and a second-life mechanic are attempts to introduce uncertainty into a format the audience has memorised. We've argued that leaning on former contestants is [more a casting admission than a bold swing](/posts/bigg-boss-20-old-vs-new-cast-admits-problem), and that tension — familiarity as both the franchise's biggest asset and its central problem — is the thing worth watching this season.

## Who is in the Bigg Boss 20 contestant list?

This is the part to treat carefully. Multiple outlets have published contestant lists this month naming names like Mahhi Vij, Geeta Basra, Jannat Zubair, Faisal Shaikh, Nia Sharma, Pearl V Puri, Yukti Kapoor, Rajat Bedi, and Jennifer Winget as approached or in talks — but as of this writing, none of this has been officially confirmed by the makers or the celebrities themselves. Contestant rumor cycles are a near-annual ritual for this show, and lists tend to shift right up until the grand premiere episode. Treat every name on these lists as "reportedly linked," not locked in, until Colors or JioHotstar confirm it directly.

It's worth understanding why these lists are so unreliable rather than just distrusting them. Casting a season this size involves approaching far more people than will ever appear, so "in talks" is technically accurate for a large group of names that were never close to signing. Fees, dates, and other commitments collapse deals late. And because a leaked name generates coverage for both the outlet and the celebrity's team, there's little cost to a name circulating that goes nowhere.

The practical read: a name appearing across several outlets means someone was genuinely approached. It tells you almost nothing about whether they'll walk into the house.

## Are the regional Bigg Boss seasons launching too?

Production house Banijay Asia is reportedly planning to launch all six regional editions of Bigg Boss around the same festive season window this year — which, if accurate, would make this one of the franchise's biggest simultaneous releases to date across languages.

The logic isn't hard to follow. The festive window is when television and streaming viewership peaks in India, and running every language edition at once turns Bigg Boss from a show into a season-long event across the whole country. The risk is equally obvious: six simultaneous editions compete for the same finite attention and the same production and marketing resources, and a franchise stretched that thin has less room for any single edition to underperform quietly.

## What should you watch for at the premiere?

The launch episode is where reporting either gets confirmed or falls apart. Three things to watch:

**The house tour.** Bigg Boss traditionally walks viewers through the set at launch, which is where you'll see whether the new art directors have genuinely rebuilt it, and whether there's a second living space consistent with the Extra Jeevan Daan reports.

**The entry order.** If the "Old vs New" theme is real, expect returning contestants to be introduced as a distinct bloc rather than mixed in — the framing tells you whether it's a structural divide or just casting.

**Which rumoured names actually walk in.** The gap between the circulating lists and the real cast is the single best measure of how much to trust this year's pre-season coverage.
