**Bigg Boss 20 premieres on September 6, 2026, with Salman Khan returning as host, streaming on JioHotstar ahead of its Colors TV broadcast. The season is built around a reported "Old vs New" theme and a redesigned house from new art directors — while every contestant list circulating online remains unconfirmed.** Makers are billing it as a landmark 20th season, and with the launch just days away, here's a straight rundown of what's actually settled versus what's still rumor.

## Who designed the Bigg Boss 20 house?

For the first time in years, the iconic Bigg Boss set won't be built by longtime designer Omung Kumar and his wife Vanita Garud Kumar. Art directors Kanchan and Rupali — who previously designed the set for *The 50* — have reportedly taken over for season 20, meaning the house itself will likely look meaningfully different from what longtime viewers are used to.

## Where will Bigg Boss 20 stream?

Reports indicate the new season will follow a digital-first model, with episodes streaming on JioHotstar before their Colors TV broadcast — a shift that mirrors the streaming-first push seen across other reality formats this year, including [India's Got Latent's own dual-platform approach](/posts/season-2-played-it-safe-not-a-compliment).

## What is the Bigg Boss 20 theme?

The headline format twist this season is reportedly an "Old vs New" structure, mixing fresh celebrity contestants with former Bigg Boss participants competing under the same roof. Alongside that, reports point to an "Extra Jeevan Daan" twist — effectively a second-life mechanic giving eliminated contestants a shot at re-entering the house through additional tasks. We've [broken down exactly how that twist is expected to work](/posts/what-is-extra-jeevan-daan-bigg-boss-20-explained) in a separate explainer, and argued that the "Old vs New" theme is [less a bold format swing than a casting admission](/posts/bigg-boss-20-old-vs-new-cast-admits-problem).

## Who is in the Bigg Boss 20 contestant list?

This is the part to treat carefully. Multiple outlets have published contestant lists this month naming names like Mahhi Vij, Geeta Basra, Jannat Zubair, Faisal Shaikh, Nia Sharma, Pearl V Puri, Yukti Kapoor, Rajat Bedi, and Jennifer Winget as approached or in talks — but as of this writing, none of this has been officially confirmed by the makers or the celebrities themselves. Contestant rumor cycles are a near-annual ritual for this show, and lists tend to shift right up until the grand premiere episode. Treat every name on these lists as "reportedly linked," not locked in, until Colors or JioHotstar confirm it directly.

## Are the regional Bigg Boss seasons launching too?

Production house Banijay Asia is reportedly planning to launch all six regional editions of Bigg Boss around the same festive season window this year — which, if accurate, would make this one of the franchise's biggest simultaneous releases to date across languages.

We'll be tracking confirmed contestant announcements and the first look at the redesigned house as the premiere date approaches.
