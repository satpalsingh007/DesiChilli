**Dulquer Salmaan's I'm Game opened on September 3 to Rs 5.40 crore net in India, per Sacnilk, edging out Bethlehem Kudumba Unit's Rs 4.90 crore Day 1 from a few weeks earlier — but the film's second-day trend suggests it's settling closer to a modest opener than the franchise-scale launch its promotion leaned into.**

## How did I'm Game's opening actually compare?

The Day 1 numbers favor I'm Game on paper: Rs 5.40 crore against Bethlehem Kudumba Unit's Rs 4.90 crore. But the context matters — I'm Game played across 2,714 shows to get there, more than the roughly 1,507 shows Bethlehem Kudumba Unit needed for its own opening, and BKU actually posted stronger occupancy at 58% against I'm Game's 43%. A wider release picking up a slightly bigger number isn't the same as playing to fuller houses.

## Why is the Day 2 trend the more telling number?

Trade tracking on Friday, September 4 — Day 2 for I'm Game — pointed to a slower start, with early estimates suggesting the day would land somewhere in the Rs 4-5 crore range. That would put I'm Game behind Bethlehem Kudumba Unit's own Day 2 figure of Rs 7.15 crore, a meaningful gap opening up right as a film's second day is supposed to build rather than plateau. It's also landing in roughly the same territory as King of Kotha, Dulquer's 2023 Malayalam opener, which posted a Rs 6.85 crore Day 1 of its own.

## Does that matter for a film Dulquer is calling a franchise starter?

It's worth flagging because of how this film was pitched going in. Dulquer has spoken about I'm Game as his most expensive production yet and framed it explicitly as the start of a planned cinematic universe — language that sets expectations well above a standard genre opener. Landing in the same range as King of Kotha, a film with no franchise ambitions attached, isn't a disaster by any measure, but it's a noticeably quieter start than the "building a universe" framing implied. The real test now is whether reviews and word of mouth can pull the second weekend up, the same pattern that turned [Hanuman Ansh's quiet opening into a runaway hit](/posts/hanuman-ansh-proves-big-budgets-arent-the-point) a few weeks earlier.
